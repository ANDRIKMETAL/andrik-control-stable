1) OneSignal delivery correctness

     successful/received (important for chrome_web_push reports).

   - diagnostic log now prints platformSuccessful/platformReceived.

2) Subscriber false-positive fix
   - channel subscriber TOTAL is authoritative.
   - if total does not rise, newly visible/public subscriber profiles are silently
     learned and DO NOT generate "New subscriber" pushes or queue entries.
   - on a real +N rise, at most N named public subscribers may be notified;
     remaining delta is covered by one generic +N push.
   - old speculative visible-subscriber credit is cleared so a future real +1
     cannot be swallowed.
   - extra public profiles beyond the actual count delta are silently seeded.
   - current public-subscriber snapshot is persisted for future unsubscribe naming.

3) Cadence/UI correctness
   - LIVE comments/chat remain every 2 minutes.
   - likes now sample about every 5 minutes (was ~10 minutes).
   - subscribers/unsubscribers remain about every 5 minutes.
   - Service UI now states the real cadence instead of implying likes are every 2 minutes.
