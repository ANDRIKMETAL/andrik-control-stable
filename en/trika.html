TRIKA

Knowledge carried through millennia

An ancient living tradition

Trika is not a modern idea or a new philosophy. Its worldview draws on older Indian spiritual traditions in which knowledge was passed from teacher to student for centuries — first orally and later preserved in manuscripts, including those written on palm leaves. Across generations, this carried an understanding of the one Consciousness, freedom and the true nature of the human being.

01

What is Trika?

Trika is an ancient non-dual spiritual and philosophical tradition of Kashmir Shaivism. It points to all existence as a manifestation of one boundless Consciousness.

The world is not separate from us. We are not separate from Life.

At the heart of everything, one truth remains: I AM.
