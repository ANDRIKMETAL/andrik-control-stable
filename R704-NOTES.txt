ANDRIK R704 — FOUR LIVE EQUALIZERS
Date: 2026-08-27
Base: R703-FOUR-VISUAL-CYCLES-MORNING-R702-PRESERVED-SAFE-FULL

PURPOSE
Add a subtle continuously animated equalizer line directly into the four normal radio visual periods.
No CapCut equalizer is needed.

POSITION
- Current track title remains at y=h-188.
- Live equalizer occupies the empty strip around y=h-108..h-82.
- Ticker remains at y=h-58.
- Therefore the equalizer is below the song title and above the ticker.

FOUR AUTOMATIC STYLES — Europe/Bratislava
- MORNING 06:00–12:00: morning-soft-gold — warm, slow, low bars.
- DAY 12:00–18:00: day-steel — brighter, slightly more active steel-white bars.
- EVENING 18:00–24:00: evening-amber — warm amber, deeper motion.
- NIGHT 00:00–06:00: night-blue — cool blue-white, slowest and calmest motion.

BEHAVIOR
- Motion is procedural and continuous, so it never freezes when MP3 changes.
- It is intentionally independent of real audio, which protects the stable R702 audio handoff path.
- It appears on MORNING/DAY/EVENING/NIGHT base streams.
- It disappears during full video clips so music videos remain clean.
- The active equalizer period/style is exposed in /status.

PRESERVED
- R703 four visual periods and scheduler.
- R702 persistent publisher, MP3 cache fix, clip→MP3 preload/handoff and silence bridge.
- AUTO FIT 1920x1080, complete source frame, crop OFF.
- QR, current-track title and ticker positions.
