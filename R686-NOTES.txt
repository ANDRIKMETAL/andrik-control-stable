R686 — YOUTUBE LIVE EMOJI PUSH FIX
Base: R685

1) YouTube LIVE custom emoji tokens in chat notifications are now converted to ordinary Unicode emoji before OneSignal delivery.
2) Example fixed: :hand-pink-waving: -> 👋, so repeated YouTube waving emojis display as 👋👋👋 instead of raw token names.
3) Added conversions for common YouTube LIVE reactions: 👍 👎 ❤️ 😍 🤩 😭 🥹 😅 🙈 ✋ 🤞 🙌 🔮 🏆 and others.
4) Future YouTube colored custom-emoji variants use semantic fallbacks when their names are recognizable.
5) Unknown YouTube-style colored custom-emoji tokens are removed from the push instead of cluttering the notification. Ordinary literal :word: text is preserved.
6) Change is limited to LIVE chat text normalization in _worker.js. R685 site, Revolut support footer, radio, OVH controls, OneSignal subscription behavior and all other functions are preserved.
7) Added cache-reset-v55-00-final-r686.html; it preserves push/service workers.
