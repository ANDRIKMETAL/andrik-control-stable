R732 — R729 STABLE TRANSPORT + TITLE LOCK + PREVIOUS/NEXT + NO ARGB

WHY
- Diagnostics showed publisher alive, lastError empty and YouTube health good.
- CPU visible processes were not saturated.
- Repeated FFmpeg warning: No accelerated colorspace conversion found from yuv420p to argb.
- R731 also allowed the raw PCM input to queue far ahead; this explains CURRENT switching roughly 20–30 seconds before the song heard on YouTube actually ended.

FIX 1 — TRANSPORT
- Restore R729/R721 proven H264 input queue: 1024 packets.
- Raw PCM queue is bounded to 8 packets (~0.74 s at 44.1 kHz stereo in FFmpeg raw packetization; roughly ~1 s including pipe buffering).
- FIFO 8192, timeshift 6 s, H264 SETTS, 4500k CBR, AAC 128k and ONE RTMPS are unchanged.
- This prevents the radio loop/UI from getting tens of seconds ahead of audible audio without starving the video feeder like R730's 64-frame H264 queue.

FIX 2 — CURRENT / PREVIOUS / NEXT
- CURRENT is static for the lifetime of the current MP3 feeder.
- PREVIOUS and NEXT are computed before the feeder starts.
- FFmpeg drawtext enable=between(t,...) shows them from T-8.0 s to T-0.30 s.
- Node wall-clock timers do not control their visibility.

FIX 3 — VIDEO STUTTER / SWSCALER
- EQ converted to yuva420p and overlay forced to yuv420.
- QR and CTA also use yuva420p -> yuv420 overlay.
- Removes the repeated yuv420p -> argb software colorspace conversion path seen in diagnostics.

FIX 4 — FADE
- 1.25 s fade to full black and 1.25 s fade from black.
- RTMPS publisher stays open.

- R729 direct special upload
- R728 single agent / no full-fit error / ADMIN_KEY bottom
