MOTORCYCLE STAGE REWORK

1. Opening animation
- The motorcycle segment no longer starts with Líra already riding.
- Líra walks toward a parked motorcycle, reaches it, mounts it, then the chase begins.
- Controls and road motion are locked during the short intro so the action reads clearly.

2. Longer motorcycle stage
- Course length increased from 5750 to 9800 distance units.
- Top speed reduced for readability and more fighting time.
- Rival encounters are spaced across the whole route.

3. Rival logic
- Riders engage one at a time instead of crossing the screen as chaotic traffic.
- An engaged rider matches Líra's speed for a short duel.
- Rival riders have a visible punch animation and can strike Líra when riding alongside.
- After the duel window they drop away from the fight.

4. Obstacles
- Dark almost-invisible pits were replaced by high-contrast red/amber road barriers.
- Road posts are reduced, deliberately spaced, enlarged in 3D and lit.
- Obstacles now read from a greater distance and do not crowd the rival encounters.

5. Cache safety
- New immutable assets: lira-3d-r1188.js and lira-models-r1188.js.
- Service worker cache bumped to R1188.
- New cache-reset-r1188.html included.
