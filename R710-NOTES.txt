R710 — YOUTUBE STABILITY + SAFE R2 DELETE

YOUTUBE / OVH STABILITY
- Final YouTube output remains 1920x1080 / 25 fps / H.264 / AAC-LC.
- Video CBR reduced from 4500k to 4000k to leave more OVH uplink headroom during short network jitter.
- VBV buffer tightened to 6000k to smooth outgoing RTMPS bursts.
- FIFO timeshift increased from 1 s to 3 s so short uplink stalls do not immediately propagate into the live source.
- Huge 2048-frame MJPEG input queue replaced by a bounded 64-frame queue (~2.6 s at 25 fps). This prevents an old-video backlog after a temporary output stall.
- Internal MJPEG feeder quality changed from q=5 to q=8. The feeder is local only; final YouTube output is still H.264 1080p. This reduces CPU and pipe pressure on OVH.
- R707 exact-title / bounded PCM queue is preserved.
- R706 animated equalizer is preserved.
- R702 persistent publisher and MP3 <-> clip handoff are preserved.
- AUTO FIT / NO CROP is preserved.

R2 DELETE
- radio/visual-library/*.mp4 sources can now be deleted from the Video Control page.
- Active MORNING / DAY / EVENING / NIGHT slot copies are protected and remain in R2/OVH after deleting their old source file.
- Owner-uploaded radio/clips/*.mp4 have their own Delete button.
- Built-in JOY OF BEING and system slot masters are protected from these delete actions.
- Radio library refreshes every ~120 seconds and reconciles the remaining queue, so a deleted clip drops out of future rotation automatically.
